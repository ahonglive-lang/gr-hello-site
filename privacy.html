GR Hello 隐私政策

更新日期：2026-10-08

一、我们不收集任何数据

GR Hello 是一款把理光 GR 系列相机拍摄的照片与视频传输到本机浏览和保存的工具。 本 App 没有账号体系，不含统计、广告或崩溃上报 SDK，不会把您的照片、位置或相机信息 上传到任何服务器。

二、数据都在本机与相机之间流转

  • 照片与视频通过相机自带的 Wi-Fi，直接在本机与相机之间传输；
  • 如果您同时使用 iPhone 与 Mac 版本，两者之间只在局域网内传递"相机热点的名称与密码" （该投递已做会话加密），不经过互联网。

三、权限用途

  • 蓝牙：与相机建立低功耗蓝牙连接，唤醒相机并读取相机热点的名称与密码 （相机热点是隐藏网络）。
  • 定位：把当前定位写入相机，用于给照片写入地理位置。 iOS 端若要"相机开机时自动同步位置"需要"始终允许"（相机开机那一刻 App 通常在后台）； 不授权时功能降级为使用最近一次的缓存位置或跳过。macOS 端还需要定位授权才能扫描并加入 相机的隐藏 Wi-Fi（这是系统的要求）。
  • 相册：展示已保存的照片、把下载的照片保存到系统相册。
  • 本地网络：在 iPhone 与 Mac 之间传递相机热点信息。

四、本机留存的数据

App 会在本机保存"相机热点的名称与密码""已下载照片的登记表""设置项"，用于下次自动连接与避免重复下载。 这些数据只存在您的设备上，卸载 App 即全部删除。

五、儿童

本 App 不面向儿童，也不会有意收集儿童信息。

GR Hello Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-08

1. We collect no data

GR Hello transfers photos and videos from a Ricoh GR-series camera to your device for browsing and saving. The app has no account system and includes no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. It never uploads your photos, location or camera information to any server.

2. Everything stays between your device and the camera

  • Photos and videos are transferred directly between your device and the camera over the camera's own Wi-Fi.
  • If you use both the iPhone and Mac versions, they only exchange the camera hotspot's name and password over your local network (that delivery is session-encrypted). Nothing goes over the internet.

3. Why the app asks for permissions

  • Bluetooth: to connect to the camera, wake it up, and read the hotspot name/password (the hotspot is a hidden network).
  • Location: to write your location into the camera so photos carry GPS data. On iOS, "Always" is required for the automatic sync that happens when the camera powers on while the app is in the background. On macOS, location authorization is also what allows scanning and joining the camera's hidden Wi-Fi network.
  • Photos: to show your saved photos and to save downloaded photos to your library.
  • Local Network: to pass the camera hotspot information between your iPhone and Mac.

4. Data stored on your device

The app stores the camera hotspot name and password, a record of downloaded photos, and your settings — only on your device, purely to reconnect automatically and avoid duplicate downloads. Deleting the app removes them.

5. Children

This app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from children.
